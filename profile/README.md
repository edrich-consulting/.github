## It’s not your platform, it’s your process.

Edrich Consulting helps IT teams improve service, simplify work, and turn priorities into a plan. We connect people, process, and technology through practical IT service management consulting.

Our work includes:

- **Capability assessments:** understand strengths, gaps, and priorities.
- **AI readiness for service management:** identify where AI can help and what it needs to work.
- **IT asset management:** understand what you own, who manages it, and where risks lie.
- **Day-in-the-life discovery:** observe real work to find delays, workarounds, and missed handoffs.
